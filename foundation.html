The Students' Fund For students, by students.

The Students' Fund is a student-run collective dedicated to elevating the UW-Madison experience. We pool contributions to fund the initiatives that students need. Your support is more than a gift — it is a stake in our shared legacy. As a contributor, you hold a special voice helping guide our endeavors.

Today, our primary focus is investing in student entrepreneurs: providing Badgers with the resources they need to turn their ideas into thriving ventures that create opportunities for fellow Badgers. As our collective vision expands, so will our impact.
